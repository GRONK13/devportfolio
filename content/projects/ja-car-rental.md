## Overview

A full-stack, mobile-first booking management and information system built for J&A Car Rental.

## What it does

- Customer accounts with authentication
- Booking management for customers and staff
- Payment integration

## Under the hood

A React and Tailwind CSS front end backed by an Express.js API, with Prisma and PostgreSQL for data.
